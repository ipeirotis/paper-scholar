\section{Background}

Attention-based architectures have displaced recurrence in machine
translation. The Transformer's big model achieves a BLEU score of 28.4 on
the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task~\citep{vaswani2017}.
Remarkably, the Transformer required substantially more training compute
than the best prior recurrent models to reach this quality~\citep{vaswani2017}.

Pre-trained bidirectional encoders transfer broadly: BERT reaches a GLUE
benchmark score of 80.5\% while also substantially reducing annotation
costs in industrial deployments~\citep{devlin2019}.

\section{Annotation quality}

Snow et al.\ show that combining labels from a small number of non-expert
annotators can match expert-level annotation quality on several natural
language tasks~\citep{snow2008}. Adaptive task routing can reduce labeling
cost by over a third~\citep{kellner2021}.
